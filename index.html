Praxis and future unit

From Reflection to Action

Praxis in climate education.

A climate lesson can give students correct facts and still leave them with nothing to do except remember. This module uses Paulo Freire’s ideas to look at a different goal: teaching that helps students understand their world and act on it.

Unit framing question

“What forms of education can prepare us for the long haul?”

So far in the course, you have looked at our relationships with Earth’s cycles and at climate change as a complex system. This unit turns to practice: what we do as teachers, and how a classroom can become a place where students think and act together.

Module overview

Four ideas, one real classroom

Each section covers one idea from Freire and the reading. Along the way, you’ll keep coming back to one real example from the reading: a wetlands unit taught by an elementary teacher in Chile.

Each section is short: an opening question, the main points, and one quick quiz or reflection. Nothing is graded or submitted.
